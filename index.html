    Why this configuration

    Power supply calculator

    Sums your CPU, GPU, and every other component, then recommends a PSU with ~40% headroom. RAM, storage, board, cooler, and fan figures are typical averages.

    Recommended PSU

    —W

    80+ Gold or better · ~40% headroom

    How much headroom?

    Run a PSU near 100% of its rating and it runs hot, the fan spins up, and it has no room for the sudden power spikes modern GPUs throw — transients can jump well above the average draw. Leaving ~40% headroom keeps a PSU in its 50–60% sweet spot: quieter, cooler, more efficient, and it lasts longer.

    What do the 80 Plus tiers mean?

    80 Plus measures one thing — how much wall power actually reaches your parts. The rest is lost as heat. Higher tiers waste less and run cooler. This shows the power lost as heat at 50% load (115 V):

    Bronze15% heat
    Silver12% heat
    Gold10% heat
    Platinum8% heat
    Titanium6% heat

    Shorter bar = less wasted power. Efficiency is 100% minus the waste, so Gold ≈ 90% and Titanium ≈ 94%. There's no "Ruby" tier — the ladder runs Bronze → Silver → Gold → Platinum → Titanium (the newer Cybenetics standard adds Diamond above it).

    Connector safety — things to look for

    • 12V-2x6 over 12VHPWR. The original 12VHPWR connector (up to 600 W) melted on some RTX 4090/50-series and RX 9070 XT cards when not fully seated. The revised 12V-2x6 (ATX 3.1) lengthens the power pins and shortens the sense pins, so the card only powers on when the plug is fully inserted — it fixes the issue by design. Prefer an ATX 3.1 PSU with a native 12V-2x6 connector.
    • Temperature sensing (TempGuard). ASRock Phantom Gaming PSUs embed a temperature sensor in the 12V-2x6 cable and cut power around 105 °C to stop a connector from melting.
    • Per-pin current monitoring. Asus ROG Astral / TUF RTX 5090 cards add per-pin shunt monitoring with an on-card alert; MSI MAG/MPG PSUs offer a similar "GPU Safeguard+".
    • Thermal cut-off cables. Corsair's ThermalProtect cable includes a hardware thermal switch that trips before the connector can overheat.
    • Seat it fully. Most melting reports trace back to a connector that wasn't pushed in all the way. Push until it clicks, and avoid sharp bends right at the connector.

    Save on Windows — optional

    Official Windows 11 Home is ~CA$139 and Pro ~CA$199 at Microsoft. You can download the installer free from the official site — microsoft.com/en-ca/software-download/windows11 — install and choose “I don’t have a product key”. It runs unactivated with a small watermark and limited personalization until you activate in Settings → System → Activation → Change product key.

    Third-party marketplaces list $10–15 keys — often volume/MSDN or gray-market and may be revoked or region-locked. If you try one, use a seller with buyer protection and understand the risk. No extra code is needed — a valid key removes the watermark.
